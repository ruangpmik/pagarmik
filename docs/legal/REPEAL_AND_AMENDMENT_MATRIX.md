## Hierarchy and transition analysis

### Not-yet-effective rules versus adjustment periods

As of the 3 November 2026 cut-off, no instrument in the reviewed, product-relevant set was identified as promulgated but not yet in force. PMK 6/2026, PMK 13/2025, and UU 1/2023 have passed their verified commencement dates. The future dates calculated from PMK 6 Article 83 are hospital adjustment deadlines, not a delayed commencement of PMK 6 or delayed repeal of PMK 82. This is limited to the reviewed set and is not a claim that no future-dated instrument exists anywhere in Indonesian law.

1. An express repealing clause governs the named instrument from the later instrument’s effective date. A separate compliance transition may govern the regulated party’s time to adjust, but it does not postpone repeal unless the text says so.
2. A statute’s transition clause is not a universal saving of all regulations. UU 17/2023 Article 453 includes a subject-matter condition: the implementing rule must not conflict with the new Act.
3. Express repeal of a parent rule does not by itself prove every subordinate rule is repealed. Check its own express repeal status, later replacement, transitional provision, and consistency with the new higher-level rule.
4. Where an official database says “Berlaku” but a changed parent or new Act creates a potential conflict, record both facts and leave article-level harmonization open; do not silently override the official status.
5. Constitutional Court decisions modify the binding meaning/effect of affected provisions from pronouncement. Record each case separately with affected articles and operative wording; do not collapse distinct decisions into one regulation record.
